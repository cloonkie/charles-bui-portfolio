About

Texas raised. California coasting.

Hi, I'm Charles: Texas raised, now California coasting.

Growing up and building my career across different communities has given me an appreciation for the ways culture shapes how we connect. Whether it's food, music, or art, I'm drawn to experiences that bring people together and remind us what it means to feel connected.

I earned my B.B.A. in Marketing from the University of Houston and my M.S. in Digital Media Management from USC Annenberg, where I focused on user experience, audience psychology, and digital strategy. Today, I apply that background as a Media Strategist at Horizon Media in Los Angeles, building data-informed campaigns that connect brands with the right audiences at the right time.

Alongside my professional career, six years of freelance work in photography, creative production, graphic design, and community events have shaped how I approach every project. Those experiences taught me that the best creative work starts with listening, collaboration, and empathy. Every client, artist, and community has a story worth telling, and I enjoy helping bring those stories to life through thoughtful strategy and execution.

Outside of work, you'll probably find me exploring a new matcha shop, gallery, or live shows-or adding another pin to my ever-growing Apple Maps guide.
