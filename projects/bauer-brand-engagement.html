Bauer College of Business

Brand engagement grounded in student behavior.

01 — Research

Engagement needed to feel more participatory.

This project explored how Bauer's social media presence could create stronger student engagement during a period when virtual connection mattered more than ever. Using survey research and behavioral analysis, I developed a campaign framework designed to invite students into the content rather than only broadcast information to them.

02 — Activation

A test post rooted in brand rules and student behavior.

I developed a test structure for Instagram stories and posts, including campaign instructions, participation prompts, and repost mechanics. The concept needed to be clear, on-brand, and easy for students to act on while following Bauer and University of Houston visual standards.

03 — Learnings

Content testing turns intuition into a planning signal.

The results connected engagement performance back to content structure and spokesperson presence. The project strengthened my ability to translate audience behavior into actionable brand communication, including an engagement framework, audience segmentation thinking, and a performance metrics roadmap.

UH Energy

A flexible visual system for an energy community.

04 — Content System

One identity across people, programs, and events.

The UH Energy work extended University of Houston brand cues into a recognizable social system for the Energy Coalition. I created assets for leadership introductions, organizational roles, event promotion, and educational storytelling while keeping typography, color, and graphic elements consistent across formats.

05 — Application

Designed to move from feed posts to stories.

Square leadership features and event graphics worked alongside vertical Instagram stories to give the organization a cohesive presence across the platform. The modular system made it possible to highlight different people and messages without losing the connection to UH Energy or the broader university brand.
