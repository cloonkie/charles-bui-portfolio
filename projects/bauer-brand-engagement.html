Bauer Brand Engagement Project

A social media engagement strategy built on survey research, brand guidelines, content testing, and performance analysis for Bauer's digital presence.

Year
2020
Role
Brand Strategist & Social Media Researcher
Disciplines
Brand Strategy · Social Media · Analytics
Context
Bauer Virtual Engagement Fall 2020

01 — Context

Engagement needed to feel more participatory.

This project explored how Bauer's social media presence could create stronger student engagement during a period when virtual connection mattered more than ever. Using survey research and behavioral analysis, I developed a campaign framework designed to invite students into the content rather than only broadcast information to them.

WIP

02 — Approach

A test post rooted in brand rules and student behavior.

I developed a test-run structure for Instagram stories and posts, including campaign instructions, participation prompts, and repost mechanics. The concept needed to be clear, on-brand, grammatically polished, and easy for students to act on while still following Bauer and University of Houston visual standards.

WIP

03 — Learnings

Content testing turns intuition into a planning signal.

The results helped connect engagement performance back to content structure and spokesperson presence. The project strengthened my ability to translate raw audience behavior into actionable brand communication, including an engagement framework, audience segmentation thinking, and a performance metrics roadmap.
