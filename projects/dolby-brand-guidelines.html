01 — Context

Preserving recognition while making room for a new system.

This branding project focused on expanding Dolby's existing visual identity through the creation of a comprehensive guideline deck for a proposed sub-brand. The objective was to preserve Dolby's recognizable design language while establishing a distinct identity for the new initiative.

02 — Impact

A system for typography, color, hierarchy, and use.

Using Figma, I developed a complete brand guideline that outlined typography, color systems, visual hierarchy, and application standards. The project challenged me to balance brand consistency with innovation, reinforcing the importance of design systems in maintaining cohesive user experiences across platforms.
