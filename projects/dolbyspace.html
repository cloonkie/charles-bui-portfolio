01 — Context

A brand extension for immersive entertainment.

DolbySpace was a two-year graduate capstone exploring how spatial audio technology could reimagine public gathering spaces through immersive entertainment experiences. The project combined market research, ethnographic interviews, competitive analysis, and UX design to propose a new consumer-facing initiative for the Dolby brand.

02 — Impact

Turning research into a brand and product proposal.

I conducted extensive audience research, interviewed industry professionals, networked with Dolby representatives, and designed a prototype digital platform that introduced DolbySpace as a potential brand extension. Throughout the project, I evaluated emerging consumer trends, validated concepts through user research, and presented recommendations to faculty stakeholders.
