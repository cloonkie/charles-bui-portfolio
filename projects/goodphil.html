01 — Context

Reintroducing a major tradition after disruption.

Following years of disruption caused by COVID-19, Goodphil 2022 aimed to reintroduce one of the University of Houston Filipino Student Association's largest annual traditions. The three-day interregional event welcomed over 5,000 attendees, featured 13 participating organizations, multiple venues, athletic competitions, cultural performances, and community sponsors.

02 — Impact

Leading media across teams, venues, and stakeholders.

As Media Chair, I led the strategic promotion and operational execution of the event's media efforts. I managed three specialized media teams responsible for branding, photography, videography, and communications while ensuring all promotional materials reflected university policies and evolving COVID-19 safety guidelines.

Beyond creative execution, I developed sponsor relationships, expanded partnership opportunities, designed event merchandise, created a resource-focused website for members, and launched an awareness campaign that introduced organizational leaders as accessible points of contact for new students.
