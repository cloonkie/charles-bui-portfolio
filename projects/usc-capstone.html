Dolby Space Digital Media Management

A graduate capstone developing Dolby Space as a consumer-facing brand extension through audience research, experience strategy, visual identity, creative production, and digital prototyping.

Year
2024–2025
Role
Brand Strategist, Designer & Creative Producer
Disciplines
Brand Strategy · Experience Design · Digital Prototyping
Context
USC Annenberg M.S. Digital Media Management

01 — The Opportunity

Bringing Dolby into shared physical experiences.

This graduate capstone explored how Dolby's spatial audio technology could extend beyond familiar entertainment products and become a consumer-facing experience. I developed Dolby Space as a proposed brand extension centered on public gathering, live performance, and the emotional connection created through sound.

02 — Research

Live music made the technology tangible.

Audience research, industry interviews, competitive analysis, and conversations with Dolby representatives helped shape the opportunity. Live music emerged as a natural setting for making spatial audio understandable: people could experience the value of the technology through emotion and connection rather than technical explanation.

03 — Experience Strategy

A flexible concept for different kinds of connection.

I shaped an experience strategy spanning intimate acoustic sets, electronic performances, full bands, and immersive crowd moments. Each format demonstrated how the concept could adapt to different audiences and environments while keeping the Dolby promise—deeper, more memorable entertainment—at its center.

04 — Creative Direction

The audience became part of the brand story.

Creative production translated the strategy into moments designed to be felt, photographed, and shared. Performers, lighting, and audience participation worked together to show how Dolby Space could build cultural relevance through experiences people would want to remember and amplify.

05 — Brand System

Recognizably Dolby, with room for a new identity.

I extended Dolby's established visual language into a complete identity system for the proposed sub-brand. Built in Figma, the guidelines defined typography, color, hierarchy, and application standards—balancing the equity of the parent brand with a distinct design philosophy for Dolby Space.
