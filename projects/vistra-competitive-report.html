01 — Context

Making competitor media activity usable for planning.

As part of the annual business planning process, I developed a competitive landscape report for Vistra to evaluate how key competitors invested their media budgets over the previous year. Using Excel to analyze spend data and PowerPoint to communicate findings, I translated large datasets into a client-facing presentation that highlighted market activity, category trends, and strategic opportunities.

WIP

02 — Learnings

Competitive pressure is a planning signal.

By determining which media channels and competitors to prioritize, I uncovered shifts in year-over-year spending, identified markets with the highest competitive pressure, and highlighted where competitors held strategic advantages. The project strengthened my ability to transform complex media data into actionable business insights, helping inform future planning and investment recommendations for the client.
