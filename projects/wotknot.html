WOTKNOT Experiential Event

An immersive gallery event celebrating Houston's creative community through fashion, visual art, and interactive installations.

Role
Event Operations & BTS Photographer
Disciplines
Experiential Marketing · Creative Production · Vendor Coordination
Context
Houston Artist Gallery Event

01 — Context

Building an experience around Houston's creative community.

WOTKNOT was an immersive gallery event celebrating Houston's creative community through fashion, visual art, and interactive installations. The event required coordination across production, vendors, artists, and venue operations to deliver a cohesive attendee experience.

02 — Impact

Timeline discipline matters when the experience is live.

I supported event operations from setup through breakdown, documenting the event through behind-the-scenes photography while assisting with production logistics and vendor coordination. Working alongside the operations team reinforced the importance of timeline management, cross-functional collaboration, and designing memorable experiences that extend beyond the physical event itself.
